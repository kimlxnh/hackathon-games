# War of Cards

## To play war of cards
Our game is a simple cards game. It automatically generates a card for both the user and the bot from a 52-pack of cards, returning both user and computer results. The person with the higher card wins the game by default.

## Rules:

- A card will be generated aurtomatically for you and the computer
- Whoever has the highest card wins.
